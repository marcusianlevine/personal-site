## Stages

### Two roads to the same first date

## Before you say a word

Wanting to talk to her isn't creepy. Hiding your interest, or ignoring her signals, is. So be honest about why you're there, and read the room first.

| What you see | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Headphones in, turned away, buried in her phone | Busy. Not a no to you, a no to everyone. | Leave her be. |
| Mid-conversation, in a hurry, at work | Not now. If she's working, she can't walk away from you. | Chat if it's natural. Don't pursue. |
| Short answers, no eye contact after your hello | She's said no. | Say a friendly goodbye. Mean it. Leave her alone. |
| Eyes up, open to the room, lingering, looks back twice | Green light. | Say hello. Start a conversation. |
| You honestly can't tell | Unclear. | One short hello. How she responds is your answer. |

## The first two minutes

- **Say hello, with the truth.** "Hi, I noticed you and wanted to say hi. I'm \[your name\]." That's the whole opener.
- **Ask one real question.** About right now: the book, the dog, the thing she's carrying, what she's up to today. Curiosity, not a line.
- **Listen.** Reply to what she actually said, not to what you planned to say next.
	- Notice your level of attraction: how excited are you to continue the conversation with her?
	- Notice her body language: does she turn toward you, hold eye contact, ask you something back?
- **Say what you want.** If it's going well: "I'd like to continue this conversation another time. Can I get your number?"
- **If she turns you down, accept the "no" gracefully.** "No worries, have a nice day." Then actually leave.
- **If she says yes, make a clean exit.** "Great, I'll text you." Then go.

## The ladder: one step at a time

If you don't feel comfortable jumping straight to the full approach outlined above, start small. Stay on a rung until your body is bored there, then climb. Bored means trained. Start this week with three Rung 1 reps.

- **Rung 1.** Ten seconds with a stranger, no goal. Ask for a recommendation, comment on the queue, hold the door and say something polite.
- **Rung 2.** A genuine compliment to a stranger, about something she chose. The jacket, the book, the bike. Not her body. Leave without asking for anything.
- **Rung 3.** Start a conversation with a woman you're *not* attracted to. A colleague, a neighbor, the person who makes your coffee. As simple as, "How's your day going?"
- **Rung 4.** Say hello to someone you're attracted to and talk. No ask. Leave when it winds down.
- **Rung 5.** The full approach above, ask included.

## The freeze, and what to do with it

The freeze before you say hi is not a character flaw. It means you care.

But that same instinct gets in the way of connection. It's the mark of an untrained nervous system. The freeze lives in your body - not your head. So just like learning a physical skill, overcoming it requires training the body.

**Before an approach:** Take a long, slow breath, then exhale longer than you inhaled. Remind your body that it's safe. Then go, while your body is settled.

**After a no:** Nothing to fix. Log it below. A no is information - not a personal judgment about you.

**After a yes:** Nice. Log it. Notice what your body did.
